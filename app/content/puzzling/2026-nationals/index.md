In addition to myself, my parents will also be competing as a pair for the first time. You can read more [about the competition](https://www.usajigsaw.org/2026-nationals).

## Friday, March 27, 2026

### 9:15am ET: Individual preliminary (Andrew)

Individual competitions are held on 500pc unreleased Ravensburger puzzles, and competitors have an hour and a half to either complete or finish as much of the puzzle as possible.

I competed in the Individual A Preliminary round, the first round of competition that determines your placement in finals. I was at table 103, and the [results are on worldjigsaw.org](https://worldjigsawpuzzle.org/usajpa/nationals2026/individual/A). I finished in 1:20:17 and secured 125th place which did not qualify me for finals, as anticipated.

The competition happened at 9:15am ET on Friday, March 27, 2026 and the recording is below.
