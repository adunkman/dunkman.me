In addition to the more normal Individual, Pairs, and Teams competitions, this championship also had two more fun competitions:

- **Pairs Relay** is a competition held on two puzzles simultaneously — you and your pairs partner work independently on the puzzles, and every 5 minutes, you switch puzzles.

- **Individual Puzzle Pyramid** is a knockout-style competition, where you start on a 100pc puzzle, and the top competitors move on. You then advance to a 200pc puzzle, then a 300pc puzzle.
